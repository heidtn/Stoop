Welcome to Stoop

A place for neighbors to talk, even when nothing else is working.

What is this?

You're connected to a small radio device called a Stoop node. It talks to other Stoop nodes nearby, passing messages along the way — kind of like neighbors passing word down the block.

Together, these little devices form a small, local network you can chat on. No cell towers, no internet provider, no accounts. Just this neighborhood, talking.

What you can do here

  • Send and read messagesChat with anyone else connected to this Stoop network.
  • Share updates and informationLet neighbors know what's going on nearby.
  • Stay in touch during outagesUseful when phones, power, or internet aren't available.

Good to know

  • No internetThis isn't connected to the internet. No browsing, email, or outside news through here.
  • It only reaches nearbyMessages travel only to other people and devices on this same Stoop network, not the whole world.
  • It can be slowMessages hop from node to node over radio. Depending on distance, that can take a few seconds — or a few minutes.
  • Short text onlyThis is a low-power radio system, not high-speed internet. It's built for small text messages, not photos or calls.
  • Not fully privateTreat it like a conversation on the porch, not a locked diary. Don't share anything you wouldn't want overheard.
  • Nodes can go offlineThese devices often run on battery or solar power. If one nearby loses power, messages may be delayed.
